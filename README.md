# Saturday 21 November 2026 — one page of world news

Entry for make.fun `news-image-20261121` ("Sum up 21 November's world news
in one image"). A newspaper front page drawn with Pillow (`draw.py` →
`entry.png`, 1024×1024, 190,222 bytes). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library
only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## The 35 stories (3 heroes + 10 secondary + 22 listed)

Regions: EUROPE 5 · AMERICAS 10 · ASIA 5 · MIDDLE EAST 5 · WORLD 3 ·
AFRICA 4 · OCEANIA 3 = 35.

### Heroes

1. **MIDDLE EAST — Iran hits US bases in Bahrain** (Reuters). The IRGC
   claims hits on two US positions at al-Riffa and Awali; Bahrain says a
   civilian industrial site was set ablaze, no injuries, and air defences
   intercepted other attacks. Iran also claims it downed a US drone near
   Qeshm Island; the US denies Iran's claim of hitting an F-16.
2. **AMERICAS — Mexico nabs FBI most-wanted boss** (AP). Special forces
   capture Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro", in Guasave,
   Sinaloa; 11 dead (4 soldiers, 7 attackers) in the raid and gunfights.
3. **AMERICAS — Peru quake 6.7 shakes the Andes** (USGS). Data table:
   magnitude 6.7, depth 108 km, epicentre 30 km NW of Junín, 00:09 UTC.
   No deaths in early reports; felt in Lima.

### Secondary

4. **EUROPE — Double-tap strike kills boy, 7** (AP). Missiles hit a
   restaurant in Kryvyi Rih, then drones struck rescuers; 2 dead, 60 hurt.
   The toll was reported Saturday; the strike itself was Friday evening.
5. **MIDDLE EAST — Three missiles downed over Riyadh** (Reuters). The
   Saudi-led coalition intercepted 3 Houthi ballistic missiles toward
   Riyadh early Saturday. (The 2 missiles over Jazan were Friday evening —
   they are NOT counted here.)
6. **EUROPE — Russian ship ablaze in Gulf of Finland** (Reuters). Two
   drones hit the cargo ship Sparta II; fire broke out, 16 crew evacuated
   to lifeboats, vessel drifted into Finnish waters; Finland says Ukraine
   is likely behind it.
7. **WORLD — COP31 runs into overtime** (Reuters). The Antalya summit
   passed its Friday deadline; the final plenary was put off to Sunday.
8. **AMERICAS — Mosquitoes cut dengue 69% in trial** (AP). Wolbachia
   mosquitoes cut dengue and chikungunya infections in children by ~69%
   in a 4-year, 5,757-child trial in Belo Horizonte; results preliminary,
   not yet peer-reviewed.
9. **WORLD — BepiColombo reaches Mercury** (AP). The ESA/JAXA spacecraft
   entered Mercury orbit after an 8-year, $2bn journey; its two orbiters
   separate 9 Dec, science starts Apr 2027.
10. **MIDDLE EAST — Strike kills three aid distributors** (Reuters).
    An Israeli strike on a truck in Gaza City; Gaza Health Ministry says
    3 killed; IDF says it targeted Islamic Jihad militants; World Central
    Kitchen says the vehicle was not affiliated with it.
11. **WORLD — Bitcoin bounces 9% over $60,000** (CoinDesk). From a
    $55,130 two-year low; $1.8bn in leveraged positions liquidated.
12. **EUROPE — Denmark urges EU curbs on Russians** (Reuters). After
    expelling Russia's defence attaché over a foiled plot to kill a
    Ukrainian rapper, Copenhagen pushes EU states to tighten Schengen
    travel for Russians.
13. **ASIA — Toxic liquor kills 11 in Manipur** (Reuters). At least 11
    dead, 180+ hospitalised after drinking adulterated alcohol.

  not collide with the "7 REGIONS / ONE PAGE" labels.

  cannot strike through the text.
- Parent's fix before submission: the numbers strip's 6th number was
  "2 tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz" with no matching story on the

  the Ethiopia ICAO story's outlet is "Capital Ethiopia" — corrected.
